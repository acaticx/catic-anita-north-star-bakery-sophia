Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to create fresh, handmade baked goods that bring neighbors together. What started as a small neighborhood bakery has grown into a welcoming place for families, commuters, and local customers to enjoy freshly baked breads, pastries, and cakes.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with quality ingredients. North Star Bakery focuses on thoughtfully sourced ingredients and works to support local suppliers whenever possible. We choose ingredients with care so that every loaf, pastry, and cake reflects our commitment to quality.

Meet Our Team

Our team of bakers and staff members brings skill, creativity, and a love of baking to the bakery every day. From preparing dough in the early morning to helping customers choose the perfect treat, our staff takes pride in being part of the neighborhood.
